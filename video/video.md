# Video

## Open Challenge rounds
**[Tezukayama Lemon's Open Challenge rounds driving video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXlHM24Sxwo)**

This is a running video of the Open Challenge rounds of Tezukayama Lemon, the Japanese representative, in the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) 2026 Future Engineers category.
In the Open Challenge rounds of this tournament, in order to run 35 seconds per lap, I created a strategy of running the fastest in the straight line at the beginning and slowing down when it comes to the corner.
In addition, by functionalizing the operation, we made it possible to quickly adjust the number during the adjustment time. And by setting a program to slow down when turning 90 degrees with a gyro, we reduced the numerical error that occurs after bending in the corner section.

## Obstacle Challenge rounds
**[Tezukayama Lemon's Obstacle Challenge rounds driving video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?si=8FFSwSAH8cAZ4jsq&v=4Pf_prBRUJY&feature=youtu.be)**

This is a running video of the Obstacle Challenge rounds of Tezukayama Lemon, the Japanese representative, in the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) 2026 Future Engineers category.
The Obstacle Challenge rounds of this tournament were mainly based on the Open Challenge rounds, and the strategy was created with a goal of 30 seconds per lap.
In addition, as a strategy, we chose to park in the parking lot. The car will depart from the parking lot. On the other hand, I didn't have time to park at first. So I decided to stop parking and improve the accuracy of other parts. In the end, the accuracy increased and I was able to secure time, so I parked.
